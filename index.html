✈️ AIRCRAFT DATABASE

Aircraft facts and specifications

✈️ Airbus A220

Type: Narrow-body passenger aircraft

First flight: 2013

Typical capacity: About 100–160 passengers

Interesting fact: The A220 was originally developed by Bombardier as the CSeries.

✈️ Airbus A320

Type: Narrow-body passenger aircraft

First flight: 1987

Typical capacity: About 140–180 passengers

Interesting fact: The A320 was one of the first airliners to introduce digital fly-by-wire controls.

✈️ Airbus A321

Type: Narrow-body passenger aircraft

First flight: 1993

Typical capacity: About 180–220 passengers

Interesting fact: The A321 is the longest member of the original A320 family.

✈️ Airbus A330

Type: Wide-body twin-engine aircraft

First flight: 1992

Typical capacity: About 250–300 passengers

Interesting fact: The A330 is designed for medium- and long-haul routes.

✈️ Airbus A350

Type: Wide-body twin-engine aircraft

First flight: 2013

Typical capacity: About 300+ passengers

Interesting fact: A large part of the A350's structure uses carbon-fibre composite materials.

✈️ Boeing 737

Type: Narrow-body passenger aircraft

First flight: 1967

Typical capacity: About 130–180 passengers

Interesting fact: The 737 family is one of the most widely used commercial aircraft families in aviation.

✈️ Boeing 777

Type: Wide-body twin-engine aircraft

First flight: 1994

Typical capacity: About 300–400 passengers

Interesting fact: The 777 was Boeing's first commercial aircraft designed entirely using digital technology.

✈️ Boeing 787 Dreamliner

Type: Wide-body twin-engine aircraft

First flight: 2009

Typical capacity: About 240–330 passengers

Interesting fact: The 787 makes extensive use of composite materials to reduce weight.

👨‍✈️ ABOUT ME
Aviation enthusiast • Plane spotter • Future pilot

✈️ WHO I AM

I am a huge aviation enthusiast who loves airplanes, airports, flight tracking and learning how aviation works. I especially enjoy discovering interesting aircraft and watching flights around Bulgaria and beyond.

🛫 MY FAVORITE AIRCRAFT

✈️ Airbus A320
✈️ Airbus A321
✈️ Airbus A330
✈️ Boeing 777

📡 PLANE SPOTTING

I enjoy plane spotting and using Flightradar24 to discover aircraft in the sky. I also like watching different airlines, aircraft types and unusual flights. Varna is one of my favorite places for aviation spotting.

🎮 FLIGHT SIMULATION

I enjoy flight simulation and especially like flying Airbus aircraft. I practice procedures such as taxiing, takeoff, flaps, landing and communication with ATC.

🌍 ENGLISH & AVIATION

I am learning English and I like using aviation communication to practice it. ATC phrases, airport vocabulary and pilot communication are some of my favorite things to learn.

💻 MY WEBSITE

I created DANI CONTROL CENTER as my own aviation project. It brings together things I enjoy, such as Flight Radar, an Aircraft Database, My Fleet, a Flight Route Planner and ATC features.

👨‍✈️ MY AVIATION DREAM

My dream is to become a professional airline pilot and fly Airbus A320-family aircraft. I want to keep learning about aviation, improve my English and one day fly real passenger aircraft.

❤️ WHAT I LOVE ABOUT AVIATION

I love the sound of aircraft, airports, runways, ATC communication, flight tracking, discovering rare aircraft and learning something new about aviation. For me, aviation is not just a hobby — it is a dream.
